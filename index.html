FARMHAND'S ROUTE

Stardew Valley 1.6, two players, one farm. Community Center done by Winter 28 of Year 1, with a money engine that carries into Year 2.

VANILLA 1.6 / CO-OP
Route

ONE YEAR, SIX ROOMS

Tick steps as you go. Spring forage and the Egg Festival cannot be redone this year.

The Community Center has six rooms and every bundle item comes from a season, a place and often a time of day or a weather. Most of Year 1 is flexible; a handful of windows are not. The route puts the one-shot items first in each season, keeps one player on the field and one on the water and in the mines, and unlocks the rooms in the order that pays back fastest: the Vault money for the bus, the Boiler Room for minecarts, the Crafts Room for the Quarry, the Pantry for the Greenhouse.

  • Every step is one line. The cost names each item. "Why" is folded away unless you turn Pro off.
  • Ticks are saved on this device. Each phone keeps its own; a shared list needs one phone.
  • Player 1 farms, Player 2 fishes and mines. Turn co-op off to see everything as one list.

BUNDLES

Tick items as you hand them in. A bundle strikes through when it has enough.

Every bundle in the Community Center as a card: the items it takes, how many of them you need, where and when each one comes from, and the reward. One-shot items carry a red tag. Filter by room, and switch to the remixed set if your farm was created with remixed bundles; the game picks one variant per slot, so tick the ones you actually see on the wall.

FOCUS

Pick money engines. Each shows its ladder and adds steps to the route.

An engine is shown as a ladder: what you buy or build first at the top, what it turns into below, with the numbers on every rung. Whatever you pick here also adds phases to each season in the Route tab, so the ticks are shared. Two engines is plenty for Year 1; the berries carry Spring to Fall, and one of the others sets up Year 2.

LEGEND

Reference tables. Tap a section.

The tables the game never puts on one screen: every crop with its seed price and profit per day, every bundle fish with its window, birthdays with a cheap loved gift, the skills and professions, energy and luck, the calendar with its festivals and closed days, and the artisan maths.

ITEMS

Tools, machines and buildings with cost, materials and one line on what it is for.

Every tool with its upgrade tiers, every machine with its exact recipe and unlock, every building Robin or the Wizard sells with its footprint drawn to scale. Search by name or by what it does.
